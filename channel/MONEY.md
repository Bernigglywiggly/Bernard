# Money side (short take, from the v5 session)

- **Infinity table and display mirror** are the fastest earners, because they're products and don't need an
  audience. Make them on-brand: an infinity table whose LED is the channel's turquoise line (v5 used amber;
  the accent is now turquoise), and a mirror that shows a daily AI number. Filming the builds makes videos
  too. Start with one prototype of each, price at 3–4× the parts, and sell locally or on Etsy first.
- **Affiliates:** from video 1, add links for the tools actually shown (ElevenLabs, Higgsfield, Blender add-ons).
  A05 (exoskeletons) has manufacturer-affiliate potential (see `BACKLOG.md`).
- **Patreon** at about 1k subscribers: project files and early cuts. **Merch** at about 5k: print-on-demand
  of the brand marks.
- Notion HQ still ranks walk-in websites as the quickest route to £2k a month; YouTube money is 6–12 months out.
- Open offer: spec the table and the mirror (parts, costs, build guide, pricing) in a separate session.
