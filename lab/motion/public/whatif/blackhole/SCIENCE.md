# What if Earth fell into a black hole? The numbers on screen

A black hole of 10 solar masses (M = 1.989e31 kg, GM = 1.3275e21 m^3/s^2).
- Event horizon radius 2GM/c^2 = 29.5 km, so 59 km (37 miles) across.
- Tidal pull compared with the Moon's on Earth: (GM_bh / d^3) / (GM_moon / d_moon^3), with GM_moon = 4.9048e12 and
  d_moon = 384,400 km. At 4.8 million miles: about 33,000 times. At 1 million miles: about 3.7 million times.
- The stretching across Earth (2 GM R_earth / d^3) equals Earth's surface gravity (9.81 m/s^2) at
  d = (2 GM R / g)^(1/3) = 1.2 million km, about 745,000 miles: beyond that, Earth's own gravity can't hold it together.
- Part of the debris from a tidal disruption falls in and forms a disk (a ring) around the black hole; the rest is
  flung away.
