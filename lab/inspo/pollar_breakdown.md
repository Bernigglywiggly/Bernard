## Why it works
- **Information gap:** how does a man refused permission to stand become PM 176 days later? You stay to close it.
- **Credibility by specificity:** dates, counts, quotes and sources on screen, every time.
- **Progress you can see:** the spine fills in, so the payoff feels near.
- **A hidden rule of the world:** the viewer leaves knowing a mechanism (the payroll vote, the ceiling of 95) that explains more than this one story. That's the brain reward.
- **Closure:** the last two lines mirror each other and snap shut.
