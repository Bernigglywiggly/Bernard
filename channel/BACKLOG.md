## A02: If war comes: the real plan

**Titles to test:** "The Government Is About to Tell You to Stockpile. Here's the Real List." ·
"What to Actually Do If the Missiles Start" · "The 72-Hour Plan Nobody Gave You"
**Thumbnail:** W1 PRESS poster, huge "72:00:00" in Unbounded, a tin can and a battery radio as
ink pictograms, the amber line running through them. No flames, no soldiers, no real footage.
**Length:** 14–16 min. **Heat:** 1. **Tone:** calm cousin who's done the reading, not a doom-prepper.

**Why now (verified):**
- The US–Israel war with Iran began in February 2026 and was on day 209 on 24 Sep; Iran still
  launches drones and ballistic missiles at US and allied sites, and says it has kept most of its
  missile production ([GlobalSecurity](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/ops/iran-war-oprep.htm),
  [CSMonitor](https://www.csmonitor.com/World/Middle-East/2026/0925/iran-trump-war-hormuz-arsenal)).
- The UN reports a "new intensity" to Russian attacks on Ukraine, and Houthi advances in Yemen
  ([UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/09/1168427)).
- On 25 Aug 2026 the Guardian reported that the Cabinet Office is preparing a campaign telling UK
  households to keep tinned food and bottled water at home; it hasn't launched yet
  ([summary](https://ukprepperhub.co.uk/government-emergency-kit/)). **This is the hook:** get ahead
  of the government's own announcement.

**Outline**

| Time | Chapter | Register / world | What it says |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Hook | FACT W0 → PRESS | "The government is about to tell you to stock food. Here's why, and the list they'll give you is missing the good stuff." |
| 0:50 | Is this actually likely? | DEEP W2 CLOUD | Honest odds. A war *reaching* your street is unlikely; *disruption* (power, fuel, prices, supply chains) is very likely, and it's the same kit either way. That reframe is the whole video: prepare for disruption, and you're prepared for worse. |
| 2:30 | The 72-hour kit | TRANSLATE W1 PRESS | Official core: water, no-cook food, torch, batteries, radio, meds, first aid, phone power ([UK guidance roundup](https://thecareercafe.co.uk/blog/uk-government-emergency-preparedness-list-what-supplies-should-you-stockpile-at-home/)). Street units: 3 litres of water a day is "two big bottles of Lucozade per person per day". |
| 4:30 | Food that works when nothing works | TRANSLATE + JOKE | Calorie-dense, no cooking, long shelf life: tinned fish, beans, peanut butter, oats, UHT milk, nuts, honey, rice. Why each works (calories per £, no water or fuel needed). "Store what you eat, eat what you store." The joke tangent: the 40-year-old tin of spam a real prepper ate. |
| 6:30 | The clever edge | FACT, fast | The stuff nobody lists: cash in small notes, paper copies of numbers, an offline map, a wind-up radio, a family meet-point, filling the bath when warnings start, knowing where your water stopcock is, a power bank per person, prescriptions topped up 7+ days ahead. |
| 8:00 | If there's a nuclear warning | DEEP, slow | FEMA's three rules: **get inside, stay inside, stay tuned** ([FEMA](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_oet_nuclear-detonation-preparedness-communicating-aftermath_052024.pdf)). Best shelter is the middle of a building: a basement, or a windowless middle floor. Fallout is most dangerous in the first hours and fades fast (the 7:10 rule of thumb). Potassium iodide protects only the thyroid, only against radioactive iodine, and only when officials tell you to take it ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/radiation-emergencies/treatment/potassium-iodide.html)). |
| 10:00 | Drones and missiles | FACT + CHROME | Civilians don't "counter drones"; they avoid being near targets (military sites, energy and fuel infrastructure), take cover on alerts, stay off roofs and windows, and never film air defence (Ukraine's hard lesson). Interception is the military's job. Here the cliffhanger: lasers now kill drones for about £10 a shot ([DragonFire](https://www.armyrecognition.com/news/navy-news/2026/uk-confirms-dragonfire-laser-weapon-deployment-on-type-45-air-defense-destroyers-by-2027), [Iron Beam](https://interestingengineering.com/military/worlds-first-combat-ready-100kw-laser)), which sets up A03. |
| 11:30 | How not to lose your mind | DEEP, HOME colours | Routine, light, sleep, a news diet (two check-ins a day), and a job for every person. What submariners and people in the Blitz did: people sang in the Tube. Community beats kit. |
| 13:00 | After | FACT | Check on neighbours, especially the old and the alone; share, don't hoard; how systems come back. |
| 14:00 | Ending: HOME | HOPE | "Being ready isn't fear. It's the opposite: it's what lets you stop worrying about it." One quiet line about looking after each other. |

**Absurd and fantasy beats (the Riley bits):** the billionaire bunker (a New Zealand compound with a
cinema) versus your bunker (the understairs cupboard, with a Pot Noodle). The "Roman soldier's
72-hour kit vs yours" split screen. A 2-second cutaway of a prepper's garage with 400 tins of beans.

**Set-pieces (W5 SHELTER):** an isometric PRESS cutaway of a house with the "best shelter" glowing
amber; a CHROME decay curve for the 7:10 rule; a W4 SIGNAL dithered drone swarm crossing a map
that the amber line routes around; the Blitz moment as grainy point-cloud people singing.

**Monetisation guard rails:** no real combat footage, no gore, no naming a current war in the title,
no "WW3" in the thumbnail. YouTube can temporarily limit ads on content tied to live conflicts
([YouTube guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278?hl=en)), so this is framed as
evergreen preparedness. Worst case it's a growth video with limited ads; it still pays for itself in
subscribers.

## A03: Robots just took a position in a real war

**Titles:** "Robots Just Took a Position in a Real War" · "Call of Duty Is Real Now" ·
"The First War Where Robots Don't Bleed"
**Thumbnail:** a chrome humanoid silhouette in W6 FRONT, the channel HUD turned into a game HUD
("NO HUMANS · BATTERY 63%"), the amber line as the aiming path.

**The facts that do the work:**
- In April 2026 Ukrainian forces captured a Russian position using only drones and ground robots;
  robotic systems flew 22,000+ frontline missions in three months
  ([Army Recognition](https://www.armyrecognition.com/news/army-news/2026/ukraine-uses-ground-robots-and-drones-to-capture-russian-positions-without-troop-losses),
  [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/04/20/europe/robots-ukraine-battlefield-drones-intl-cmd)).
- About 280 Ukrainian companies make 550+ ground-robot models; the MoD is contracting 25,000 ground
  robots in H1 2026. Humanoid Phantom MK-1s (1.8 m) went to Kyiv, and Ukraine is funding armed
  humanoid development ([Defense Post](https://thedefensepost.com/2026/07/03/ukraine-humanoid-combat-robots/),
  [Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/07/12/ukraine-is-first-country-to-fund-combat-humanoid-robots-but-its-own-battlefield-says-wheels-still-win/)).
  The funny truth: on the real battlefield, wheels still win.
- Laser air defence is live: Iron Beam's first combat interceptions in March 2026 at a marginal cost
  of dollars per shot versus $100k+ for an interceptor missile
  ([Interesting Engineering](https://interestingengineering.com/military/worlds-first-combat-ready-100kw-laser)).

**Spine:** how war changed (spears → gunpowder → planes → drones → software), what's real now,
what's next (2030, 2040), and the Terminator scale from "robot carries water" to "no human on either
side". The absurd end-state: a war that's just two server bills; whoever has better batteries
wins. The deep line: when nobody bleeds, is it easier to start? **HOME:** the same machines are
demining farmland and pulling people out of rubble; the best robot of 2040 is a rescue robot.

**Set-pieces:** a CHROME humanoid march through fog; the capture reconstructed in CLOUD points
(drones as sparks, robots as solid shapes); the HUD literally becoming a game HUD;
"real vs Call of Duty" split screens in PRESS.

## A04: AI was the #1 reason for layoffs this year. Now what?

**Titles:** "AI Was the #1 Reason for Layoffs This Year. Now What?" · "The Jobs AI Is Actually
Taking (It's Not the Ones You Think)" · "Your First Job Just Got Harder. Here's the Edge."
**Thumbnail:** "116,175" huge in PRESS, a pay slip stamped "REPLACED?" with the question mark in amber.

**Facts (translate every one):**
- AI was the leading stated reason for US layoffs in 2026: 116,175 cuts through August
  ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ai-layoffs-hiring-entry-level-workers/)).
- Employment for 22–25-year-olds in AI-exposed jobs is down about 13% since late 2022
  ([Stanford SIEPR](https://siepr.stanford.edu/publications/policy-brief/what-really-happening-jobs-separating-ai-hype-reality)).
- The nuance that makes us trustworthy: some "AI layoffs" are really cost-cutting to pay for AI, or
  post-pandemic over-hiring; the bigger effect is juniors not being hired at all.

**Spine:** what's real, what's hype, who's exposed, who's safe, then **the edge**: the junior who
works like a three-year veteran because they run AI properly; the skills that go up in value
(judgement, taste, trust, hands-on trades). The absurd beat: "the reverse centaur", where the AI does
the job and you do the AI's paperwork. **HOME:** every big technology killed jobs you can name and
created jobs nobody could have named yet.

**Why it earns:** careers, money and skills pull finance-adjacent advertisers (UK tech/finance CPMs
sit among the highest; [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/most-profitable-youtube-niches/)).

## The formula (every video)

1. A 50-second hook built like A01: a fact, a translation, a joke, a deep line, cut on the key syllables.
2. Worlds follow the register; the hero world arrives at the midpoint as the "whoa" set-piece.
3. **The stoner test:** every 2–3 minutes a shot you'd rewind, one cosmic zoom-out per chapter, and
   tangents that pay off. Hypnotic, never lazy.
4. Absurd and real side by side: the Terminator beat next to the real thing, labelled honestly.
5. One Huey line per video: the longest, calmest shot.
6. End in HOME, hopeful, with a small nod to the community ("see you on the line").
7. Heat 1. Facts exact and sourced on screen as micro labels (they also build trust).
