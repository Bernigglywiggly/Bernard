How long should my YouTube titles be?

We analyzed 300,000+ viral YouTube videos from this year.
Here’s what we found:

1️⃣ Shorter titles win. Consistently.

Titles with ≤6 words massively outperform everything else.

Once you pass 10+ words, median views fall off a cliff.

2️⃣ Why? The YouTube homepage cuts you off.

Long titles get cut off.
Cut off titles kill readability and curiosity
Readability and curiosity drive CTR.
CTR drives everything.

3️⃣ The character count data is even crazier.

At ~30 characters → median views ≈ 65K

At ~70 characters → median views ≈ 40K

That’s roughly a 62% drop in performance…
…just because the title got longer.

4️⃣ “But my idea needs context.”

No. Your thumbnail carries the context.

Keep your thumbnails simple, but use them to tell a story, more than your title can.

5️⃣ The winning format looks like this:

Short 

Punchy

One clear tension

Thumbnail does the heavy lifting

6️⃣  The shift is simple:

Stop asking:
“Does my title explain everything?”

Start asking:
“Does my title spark a click?”

Those are different goals.
One grows channels. One doesn’t.

8️⃣ Final takeaway

Shorten your titles.
Raise your ceiling.
