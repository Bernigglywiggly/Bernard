## EP01 · Sixteen Hours  *(being built: `lab/ep01/`)*
- **Ground:** "This task takes a human expert sixteen hours. In March 2026 an AI finished tasks like it half the time, on its own." [V: METR, ≥16 h 50% horizon] "Seven years earlier, the best it could manage took you two seconds." [V: METR 2025, GPT-2-era ≈ 2 s]
- **Mechanism:** METR times people on ~228 tasks (seconds to days), gives the same tasks to models, finds where they win half the time: the *time horizon*. [V] Ladder: 2 s (2019) → ~30 s (2022) → ~4 min (Mar 2023) → ~1 h (early 2025) → 16 h (Mar 2026). [V: METR 2025 paper + METR Mar 2026]
- **You:** 16 hours = two working days: coursework, a takeaway's website, the mod you never finished.
- **Idea:** exponential blindness. Fold paper 42 times and it passes the Moon. [M: 0.1 mm × 2⁴² ≈ 440,000 km] Doubling every ~7 months since 2019, lately ~4. [V]
- **Imagine:** if it held: a working week by the end of 2026, a working month by mid-2027 [M, I]. What would you hand over? What would you keep?
- **Close:** "It took six years to get from two seconds to an hour. It took one more to get from an hour to sixteen."

## EP02 · The Ninety-Minute War  *(built: `lab/ep02/`; as made, the IDEA is the Red Queen and IMAGINE asks what gets expensive)*
- **Ground:** on 22 Sep 2026 two labs launched new models about 90 minutes apart, and both cut prices. [V, the 90 minutes is single-source]
- **Mechanism:** why launches cluster: nobody wants to own the news cycle second; price is the only lever everyone can read.
- **You:** what a million tokens is in your units; what your side project costs now.
- **Idea:** the **Red Queen** (Van Valen, 1973): in evolution, species run to stay in place. Labs now do the same. [C]
- **Imagine:** a genius costs 8p an hour. What happens to advice, tutoring, law, medicine, when anyone can afford the best?
- **Close:** mirror the two launch times.

## EP04 · The Number That Decides What Gets Watched
- **Ground:** Europe's AI rules presume a model is "systemic risk" above 10²⁵ training operations. [C: EU AI Act Art. 51]
- **Mechanism:** you can't measure danger, so the law counts arithmetic.
- **You:** what 10²⁵ is: every person on Earth doing a sum a second for ~40 million years. [M: 10²⁵ ÷ 8×10⁹ ÷ 3.15×10⁷ ≈ 4×10⁷ years]
- **Idea:** Goodhart's law: when a measure becomes a target, it stops being a good measure.
- **Imagine:** compute rationed like carbon, with thinking allowances and markets for spare thought.
- **Close:** mirror "counting danger" and "counting sums".

## EP05 · Why the Robot Can Pass the Exam but Can't Fold Your Shirt
- **Ground:** machines beat us at chess in 1997 [V] but struggle with the laundry basket.
- **Mechanism:** **Moravec's paradox** (1988): what's easy for us took evolution hundreds of millions of years; what's hard for us is new. [C]
- **You:** which parts of your job are "old brain" (hands, taste, reading a room)? Those last longest.
- **Idea:** difficulty is a measure of how recently we learned something.
- **Imagine:** the first robot that moves like a toddler. What changes in a care home, a kitchen, a building site?
- **Close:** mirror "chess" and "shirt".

## EP06 · The Thirty-Year Delay
- **Ground:** electric motors were in factories by the 1890s, yet productivity barely moved for decades. [C: Paul David, "The Dynamo and the Computer", 1990]
- **Mechanism:** factories were built around one giant steam shaft. The gains came when they redesigned the whole floor around small motors.
- **You:** bolting AI onto an old routine versus redesigning the routine.
- **Idea:** new power needs new shapes. The tool is never the bottleneck; the layout is.
- **Imagine:** an office designed from zero around AI. What disappears first: meetings, inboxes, job titles?
- **Close:** mirror "the motor arrived" and "the redesign arrived".

## EP07 · The Library of Every Book
- **Ground:** Borges imagined a library holding every possible book (1941). [V: "The Library of Babel"]
- **Mechanism:** a language model is a compression: it keeps patterns, not pages.
- **You:** what that means for trust: it's fluent by design, right by effort.
- **Idea:** meaning is the rare path through an almost endless space of nonsense.
- **Imagine:** a librarian who has read every shelf, sitting beside every child on Earth. What does school become?
- **Close:** mirror "every book" and "the one you need".

## EP08 · Cheaper Makes More
- **Ground:** more efficient steam engines made Britain burn *more* coal (Jevons, 1865). [C]
- **Mechanism:** when something gets cheaper, we find more uses for it than we saved.
- **You:** cheaper intelligence doesn't mean less work for you; it means more things become worth doing.
- **Idea:** the **Jevons paradox**: efficiency increases total use.
- **Imagine:** the jobs that exist only because thinking got cheap. Name three that don't exist yet.
- **Close:** mirror "less coal per engine" and "more coal in total".

## EP09 · Who's Human Here?
- **Ground:** in a 2025 study, people picked an AI as the human more often than the real human. [C: Jones & Bergen, UCSD, three-party Turing test]
- **Mechanism:** the test was never about intelligence. It's about what we expect a person to sound like.
- **You:** how to tell online now: ask for the specific, the local, the lived.
- **Idea:** Turing's real question (1950) was "can machines do what we (as thinkers) do?", not "can they fool us?" [V: 1950 paper]
- **Imagine:** a world where every voice you hear might be synthetic. What becomes precious: handwriting, meeting, silence?
- **Close:** mirror "fooled by a machine" and "fooled by our expectations".

## EP10 · Data Centres in Orbit
- **Ground:** plans to put AI chips in space, where the sun never sets. [C: Google "Project Suncatcher"; launch dates to check]
- **Mechanism:** in the right orbit a solar panel gets near-constant sunlight, and space is a cold sink, but heat has to be radiated away.
- **You:** your next question to an assistant might be answered from orbit.
- **Idea:** computation follows energy, like mills followed rivers.
- **Imagine:** a thinking ring around the Earth. Who owns sunlight?
- **Close:** mirror "mills on rivers" and "minds in orbit".
