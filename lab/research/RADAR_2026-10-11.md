## 5. Format insights: what the outliers do that we don't

| What | Outliers in our niches | Ours | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Length | Median outlier length: HTP niche **16 min**, Money Crimes **25 min**, Curve niche **12 min**, Spanish 19 min | Visa **4.3 min**, Ponzi 11.0, Curve films 9-11.6 | 25/100, 25/68, 44/180 videos ≥10× in each scan |
| Who breaks out | **Small channels**: 17 of 25 HTP and 16 of 25 Money Crimes outliers come from channels under 100k subs | We are at 0-100 subs: topic choice matters more than size | same scans |
| Title | Median 53-69 characters. MC: a **case-file collage on a map** with a route line ("The Missing Crypto Queen"). Geo: a map with a red arrow and circle | v2: a giant number on black, the place absent or secondary | contact sheets of 23 top outliers |
| First 30 s | One dated scene + the paradox number inside 20 s (the Ritz, March 2020: 136 empty rooms sold for about £800M, "£6 million per empty room"); or a school myth broken in 15 s ("oil comes from dinosaurs... not even close"); a light joke allowed ("Polar bears? 1. **Length floors**: HTP 12-16 min (8 min minimum for mid-rolls; Visa at 4.3 earns no mid-roll), Money Crimes 18-25 min with a
   cold open mid-story, The Curve 10-13 min. Add depth (a second case, the counter-argument, the "what I'd do"), not padding.
2. **Searchable title variant in every Test & Compare**: HTP "How [Company] Really Makes Money (twist)", Curve "Why [place] [odd
   fact]?", MC "[amount] + [mystery]". Run it against our current style (playbook test T2).
3. **Thumbnail variant C = the real place with the number on it**: a drawn gas station, hotel or tank farm, the money tag and an
   arrow on the detail, a lighter ground. Keep A/B on black. For MC, the case-file-on-a-map collage (our map kit already makes it).
4. **Name the belief in line 1**: "You'd think [common belief]. [Sourced fact that breaks it]." One dated scene and one paradox number
   by 0:20 (fits CRAFT §9 rules 1 and 7).
5. **Translate every map film for Curva Explica** (Hormuz first) with one native chapter for that audience (Mexico fuel prices):
   the Spanish outliers show the same topics winning at higher ratios, and the cost is a re-voice, not a rebuild.
