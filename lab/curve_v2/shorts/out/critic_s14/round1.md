# Critic round 1: Short 14 v1 (SHORT14_v1.mp4, 36.9 s)

Judged on rendered frames (contact sheets, frame0, phone.jpg, extra frames at 19-36.6 s), motion/loudness/loop files, facts file, CRAFT, RUNBOOK 8b. Limit: I could not isolate music vs voice stems, only the mix.

## Scores
1. **Hook / frame 0: 6/10.** Frame 0 is moving real footage (booster in the arms, flame) with a ring and a 4-word kicker "NOW CATCH THE SHIP?": it meets 8b. Weak points: the picture fills only y 330-1090 (40% of the height), the bottom 33% (y 1300-1920) is empty navy, so as a thumbnail it is a small clip over a void. The booster itself is ~100 px wide. Line 1 (0-3 s) "You're watching a tower catch a falling rocket" only describes the clip (CRAFT: describing loses reach); the surprise ("nobody has caught the ship") lands at ~8 s. Our drawing arrives at ~8 s, not by 2 s: the ring (0 s) and scoreboard are drawn, but a bare ring plus tick boxes is thin added value. RULE MISS (soft).
2. **Readability at 360 px: 5/10.** Kickers and scoreboard are fine (60-80 px). Problems: auto-shrunk captions "NEXT, SPACEX WANTS IT" (4.5-6 s), "TO TRY ON FLIGHT 15," (10-12 s), "STEERING WITH ITS FLAPS" (~17 s), "MEANS LESS TO CARRY," (~26 s) drop to roughly 40 px, under the 68 px floor. "SPACEX'S PLAN, DRAWN BY US" (mono, ~15 s onward) is ~36 px, unreadable on the phone sheet. "EXPECTED NOV 2026" (grey-lilac, ~12 s) is small and low contrast. "FROM ABOVE" inset label is tiny. Credit "SPACEX VIDEO · SLOWED" ~38 px mono (borderline). Safe areas are fine: nothing at x>950 apart from the scoreboard's third box at ~x 930-957 on frame 0 (just inside), tags are at x 60, subscribe plate ends y~1440 (above 1650).
3. **Motion: 6/10.** Measured frozen 0%, near-still 3%, so the stat passes, but it is flattered by captions swapping every 0.8 s. The "NEVER DONE BEFORE" scene (~8-15 s, 7 s) has two static icons: only a checkbox "?" and a stamp pop in; no drawing moves: a dead beat in spirit. "WHY CATCH IT?" legs-cross-out scene (~23-25 s) is a static rocket for ~2 s. The verdict (28-35 s, ~8 s) is a static tower with text rows adding: new item every ~2 s but nothing alive. The fall (13-18 s) and catch (18-23 s) are the only real motion.
4. **Variety / composition: 4/10.** Same layout in every scene: kicker top-left, small subject centred, caption at y~1440, dark purple gradient everywhere, bottom third empty. Subject fill is 15-25% in scenes 8-15 s, 23-26 s and 28-36 s (rule: 60-85%). Banned defaults hit: small card/subject floating in an empty frame (why-legs, verdict tower right side with an empty left), corner labels ("THE FALL / OUR DRAWING" top-left), an inset "FROM ABOVE" card, one gradient behind all. The catch drawing's ship at 18 s is ~15% of the frame width and one bar for "two giant arms" (the inset shows two).
5. **Accuracy: 7/10.** Matches: three booster catches, ship 0, Flight 15, "expected November" (row 13 U, no day stated: good), belly-first/flaps/engines/upright/between arms (row 20), no legs/under an hour (row 22), no speeds. Mismatches/risks: (a) shot spec says "four flaps twitching" but the drawing shows two flaps per side view and the script text never claims four: harmless, but "STEERING WITH ITS FLAPS" over a drawing with no visible flap motion is a weak proof. (b) "BOOSTERS CAUGHT" ticks cyan x3 while footage is only Flight 5: fine, but credit should say "FLIGHT 5" (facts file section 0 names the source). (c) "EXPECTED NOV 2026" is stamped: facts say not to claim a date; month-only is OK. (d) Drawing shows the booster a plain slab with nubs and the ship at similar height: not to scale; label "NOT TO SCALE" or fix. (e) No source credit for "Musk's goal" on screen: add "MUSK, 20 OCT CALL" if space allows. No wrong numbers found.
6. **Sound: 7/10.** Integrated -14.1 LUFS, peak -1.5 dBFS: on target. LRA 1.1 LU is very flat; per-second short-term stays -13.5 to -14.9. Bed ducks to ~-28 dB RMS in voice gaps (good, voice clearly on top). Final second drops to -16.7 (end fade): fine for a loop but the voice-less tail should be ~0.4 s, check the cut back to frame 0 has no click. I could not verify whoosh/click placement or beat alignment from the mix; no evidence of SFX on scene changes at 8, 13, 18, 23, 28 s.
7. **Authorship: 4/10.** Looks templated: kicker + small centred vector object + yellow-highlight caption on dark purple matches the other Curve Shorts. The view of the catch is drawn but generic, with no on-screen verdict beyond a text row, no measured or original number, no date/stamp that is distinctly ours. Unmistakable would be: one recurring tracked object (the ship drawn large, rotating through the whole film), our own scale annotations, and a verdict plate in a fixed Curve form.
