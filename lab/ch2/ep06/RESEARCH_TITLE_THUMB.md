# HTP 06 (Visa): title and thumbnail research (6 Oct 2026)

How it was done: vidIQ keyword data, a YouTube search of this exact topic sorted by views, vidIQ outliers, vidIQ title
scores for six candidates (55 of 150 vidIQ credits), and one web search on thumbnail click-through research. Limits are
at the end. Nothing here is a guarantee: the real test is YouTube's Test & Compare on the live video.

**A trap found:** searching "visa" on YouTube is dominated by IMMIGRATION visas (the outliers feed for "how visa makes
money" returned visa-status vlogs and H-1B news). A title that says only "Visa" will be shown to the wrong people. The
title or the thumbnail must say "card" or show one.

## 3. The psychology being used
| Lever | What it means here |
|---|---|
| Information gap (curiosity) | State something that conflicts with what the viewer believes, and withhold the reason |
| Self-relevance | "your card", "when you tap": the viewer is in the story |
| Concreteness | One specific number beats an adjective ("24¢", "$20 billion") |
| One idea per surface | Eye-tracking summaries say viewers look at the thumbnail first and longer; the title confirms. So the two must say DIFFERENT things that complete each other |
| Legibility | Three words or fewer of big type; high contrast; one focal object; readable at 168x94 px |

**Recommendation: "Visa Isn't a Credit Card Company".** One point under the top score, inside the 6-word rule, and the
only candidate that both names Visa and says "credit card". Second for the A/B: "Visa Makes $20 Billion Without Lending
a Cent".

## 5. Thumbnails (thumbs.py -> thumb_A/B/C.png, thumbs_sheet.png)
| | Big type | Pairs with the title because | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 24¢ OF EVERY $100 | the title says what Visa isn't; this says what it takes. A number the viewer has to resolve | "24¢" alone needs the card beside it to make sense |
| B | LENDS $0 / KEEPS HALF | states the contradiction outright | four words, the most to read |
| C | $17 TRILLION / NOT ITS MONEY | the film's own look as the pattern-break in a feed of stock photos | no card in shot, so "Visa" in the title carries the whole meaning |
**Recommendation: A as the main thumbnail, B and C as the other two arms in Test & Compare** (YouTube allows three).
All three keep the bottom-right corner clear for the duration stamp and have no logo.

## 6. Limits
- vidIQ's title score is a model's estimate, not measured click-through. Scores were run without a channel ID.
- The thumbnail figures quoted on the web (for example "+38% for high contrast", "faces lift CTR 20-30%") come from
  marketing blogs, not controlled studies; one peer-reviewed paper exists on sentiment in titles and thumbnails
  (Journal of Business Research, "Clicks for money"). Treat the levers as sound and the percentages as soft.
- The thumbnails were NOT scored by vidIQ (its scorer needs a published video or a hosted image) and have not been
  seen by an independent critic. This channel has no faces, which the same sources say costs some click-through.
- The channel is new: early impressions will be few, so Test & Compare may take weeks to pick a winner.
