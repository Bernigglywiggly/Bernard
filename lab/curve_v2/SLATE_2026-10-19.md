## THE FORMAT (every new Curve Short)
1. One real, credited clip of a hot news moment (official footage, 7 s windows), RIFE-slowed, vertical pan crop.
2. Hook in line 1: the question the clip makes you ask ("Upside down and not falling?").
3. One crisp Remotion diagram (or Blender x-ray) that answers it: the mechanism, drawn bright and colourful.
4. One surprising receipt (history, contract, number), labelled OUR MATH / OUR ESTIMATE where ours.
5. End on our own verdict; loop back to frame 0. 30-35 s. Chris v4 VO, whisper captions via pager.ts.
6. Footage under half the runtime. film-critic before upload. Daily upload while the story is hot.
