- **Deadline:** the Partner Programme bar doubles for new applicants on **1 Feb 2027** (8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views; YouTube blog, 10 Aug 2026). Getting in before then means 1,000 subs plus 4,000 watch hours or 10M Shorts views in 90 days.
