## Description

Last year Amazon's sales came to $717 billion, most of it shopping. But 57% of its operating profit came from a business most shoppers never see: Amazon Web Services, which rents out computers.

Amazon is really five businesses behind one website: the shop, a marketplace where other sellers pay a commission on every sale, advertising ($68.6 billion in 2025), subscriptions, and the cloud. The 2025 accounts show the shops making about 7 cents in every dollar in North America and under 3 cents elsewhere, even with the sellers' fees and the adverts counted in, while AWS made 35 cents. This is how that machine works, where your money goes when you buy from a seller, and what to look for when you search.
