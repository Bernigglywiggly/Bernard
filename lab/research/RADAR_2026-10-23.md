**Score:** D G T F E, 1-5 each = /25, rules in `RADAR_HOWTO.md`, with **F re-weighted for the clip format**:
F 5 = a striking official clip exists (company, agency, NASA, military, public domain) AND the answer is one diagrammable
mechanism; F 3 = official stills/loops or our own footage only; F 1 = no usable footage, or it needs faces or
news-network footage we can't use. Make 17+. (unv.) = not checked at a primary source. RPM tier is long-form, rough:
High = money, cyber, consumer tech; Mid = robots, space, AI; Low = weather, general news, mostly non-US audiences.
Shorts pay far less in every tier.
