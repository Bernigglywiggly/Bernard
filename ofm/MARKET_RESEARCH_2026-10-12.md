# AI-persona creator market research (Mon 12 Oct 2026)

This builds on `~/Bernard/FANVUE_PLAN_8OCT.md`. It does not repeat that file's rules, costs or 30-day plan. It adds what public sources say about what makes these accounts grow and earn.
Public sources only. Nothing was posted, created or sent.

**Evidence labels**

| Label | Meaning | How much to trust it |
|---|---|---|
| **[P]** | Platform's own page or statement (Fanvue, Instagram, YouTube, X) | High on rules. Medium on performance numbers, which have no published method. |
| **[P2]** | Platform page read through a third-party summary (mostly SirenCY, dated 1 Oct 2026) | Medium-high |
| **[J]** | Journalism or analyst (Fortune, Sacra, Business Insider, Euronews, Matthew Ball) | Medium-high |
| **[S]** | Study run by a company that sells creator tools (OnlyGuider, Supercreator) | Medium. Large samples, no audit. |
| **[V]** | Vendor, agency or coach blog | Low. Sells the thing it describes. |
| **[F]** | Forum or anonymous operator anecdote | Lowest. Unverified. |

Reddit and YouTube transcripts could not be read: Reddit blocks this research tool, and the vidIQ account has no credits. YouTube was checked through titles and descriptions only. Every AI-OFM YouTube result found was a course or funnel pitch.

## 1. The six findings that change the 8 Oct plan

| # | Finding | Number | Label | Change to the plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Paid messages, not the subscription, carry the money | Messages 69.7% of revenue, subscriptions 4.1% (1.0M OnlyFans subscribers). Fanvue's own guide says paid messages are the biggest earner in a creator's first 2 months. OnlyFans overall: over 60% of spending is per-transaction. | [S] [P2] [J] | Plan assumed subs = 50 to 60%. Model messages/PPV at 60 to 70% instead. Price the sub low and build the PPV ladder first. |
| 2 | The first 48 hours decide most of the spend | 83.3% of payments come in the 48 hours after someone subscribes. Only 4.2% of subscribers ever pay. | [S] | Every new subscriber gets a reply inside hours. Day 1, 3 and 7 messages (Fanvue's own schedule). |
| 3 | Top spenders buy conversation, not explicit content | 0.01% of subscribers gave 20.2% of revenue. 78% of top-spender chats are not about sex. | [S] | Persona needs talkable hobbies (gaming, gym, pets, shows). Chat quality matters more than more photos. |
| 4 | AI-assisted messaging goes with higher earnings | Fanvue creators using AI messaging earn 6.3x those who don't. A creator exec saw an 18% drop in sub revenue in one cycle when replies took over 48 hours. | [P] (correlation, no method), [J] quote | Use Fanvue's built-in tools. Correlation only: busy creators use more tools. |
| 5 | Bundles keep fans longest; time-limited discounts churn fastest | Fanvue's own tenure data: bundle joiners stayed longest and spent most after; trial joiners middle; time-limited discounts churned fastest. | [P2] | Default offer = 3-month bundle. Trials only per-channel and measured at first renewal. |
| 6 | Two platforms changed the rules in summer 2026 | Instagram: unlabelled AI-person profiles lose recommendations (from 31 Aug 2026). YouTube: terminated channels that linked to sexual content (late Jul 2026). | [P] [J] | IG label on from day 1 (already in plan). **Drop YouTube as a link source.** |

| In GBP at £1 = $1.33 | £1,805 |
| Less £60 tools | about £1,745 |
| Views needed at 10 to 20 subs per 100k (one-time, ignoring churn) | 0.75M to 1.5M |
| With 25 to 40% monthly churn, new subs needed each month to hold 150 | about 38 to 60 |

- So £2,000/month needs about 165 to 175 paying subs at $20, held against churn. That matches the 8 Oct plan's optimistic column (156 subs).
- The evidence does not change the plan's December odds. It changes how to run it: chat-first, PPV-first, bundles, IG + X, no YouTube.
