## The mechanism to explain (from the 10-K, to quote exactly)
- Visa doesn't lend money and doesn't issue cards: banks do. It runs the network and sets the rules.
- Interchange (the biggest slice of a card fee) is paid by the shop's bank to the cardholder's bank; Visa sets the
  default schedule but doesn't receive it. TO VERIFY wording in the 10-K.
- Cross-border payments are the most valuable: international transaction revenue $14.2B.
- The litigation provision: the US interchange "MDL" case. Find the current state of the merchant settlement.

## Angle (CRAFT §1)
"The company that never touches your money": Visa keeps about a quarter of a cent per dollar and turns half of its
revenue into profit, because it runs the rails and lets the banks carry the risk. A follow-the-dollar structure
would repeat Film 05, so use a map instead: one card tap traced across the network (shop, acquirer, Visa,
issuer), with each hop's cut.
