## Facts (V = two independent sources agree; P = primary; read 31 Oct 2026)
| Row | Fact | Grade | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Campione d'Italia is an Italian town completely surrounded by Switzerland (the canton of Ticino) and Lake Lugano | V | [Wikipedia: Campione d'Italia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campione_d%27Italia); [The Local, 26 May 2019](https://www.thelocal.it/20190526/high-stakes-for-enclave-as-europes-biggest-casino-goes-bust) |

| 3 | Its everyday currency is the Swiss franc (the euro is legal tender and accepted); its phones use Switzerland's +41 code (except the town hall, +39) | V | Wikipedia (citing miralux.ch, valentinacarbone.it); The Local (row 1) |
| 4 | Its casino, the Casinò di Campione, was declared bankrupt by a court in Como on 27 July 2018 and closed | V | Wikipedia: Campione d'Italia; [Wikipedia: Casinò di Campione](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casin%C3%B2_di_Campione); The Local |
| 5 | The casino's takings paid for the town (a 1933 decree ties its proceeds to the municipality's costs); when it shut, the town fell into crisis, with creditors from pizzerias to the fire department | V | The Local (AFP, 2019); The Telegraph (Andrea Vogt, 2019), as cited by Wikipedia |
| 6 | It re-opened on 26 January 2022 | V | Wikipedia (citing QuiComo); [FIRSTonline: Casino di Campione reopens after 3 years](https://www.firstonline.info/en/Casino-di-Campione-reopens-its-doors-after-3-years-of-hiatus/) |
