## Description

Last year McDonald's collected $10.4 billion in rent: more than its entire profit of $8.6 billion. Not from you. From the people who run its restaurants.

About 95% of McDonald's restaurants are run by franchisees. They pay for the kitchen, the staff and the food, then pay McDonald's twice: a royalty for the name, and rent on a building McDonald's owns or leases. The rent is a share of sales, with a minimum, so McDonald's is paid before anyone knows whether the restaurant made money. This is how that machine began (Ray Kroc's 1.9% and Harry Sonneborn's real-estate idea), what the 2025 filings show, and what it means for anyone thinking of buying a franchise.
