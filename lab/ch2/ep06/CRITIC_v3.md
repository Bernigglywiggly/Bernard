## 4. Verdict

Not publishable yet, and that is the picture speaking, separate from the content items the builder left open. v3
did real work: red is finally used once and stays in shot, the lit cell's labels sit inside their boxes, the quote
is whole, the leftover text in the meters scene is gone, the last chapter's column no longer scrolls into the top
edge, and the illustration note can be read. But claim (b) is not true: every one of the three transits still shows
chopped giant glyphs, a headline sliced by the frame edge on the way in and out of the new overview, and the first
one still passes through 0.6 s of pure black at 0:29. The fade-out fix opened a new hole, about ten seconds of
almost nothing at 1:33-1:43 while the voice explains a meter that is not drawn. The last chapter is balanced but
too small and clips its first node for 37 seconds. Nineteen of forty earlier items are untouched (small mono notes,
the four-parties boxes in an empty frame, the static 42 s close-up, caption cross-fades, the café label behind the
plate). One more pass would do it: route each transit as a single direct move that never leaves a lit object (or
hold the overview 2 s and enter and leave it at overview scale), fill 1:33-1:43, scale and reframe 3:30-4:07, and
fix V2 and V3. The spoken interchange claim (item 4) remains a hard stop on its own.
