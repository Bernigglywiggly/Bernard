## ⚠️ Read before publishing

1. **Don't claim "icon pack for Nova / Niagara / Lawnchair".** The icons are PNG files. Niagara and Lawnchair only take icons from *installed icon-pack apps (APKs)*, and Lawnchair's docs confirm per-app icons come only from installed packs. Nova does allow per-app gallery images, but its founder left in Sept 2025 and active development stopped. The copy below says "works on Android with a free shortcut app or any launcher that supports custom icon images", which is true. If you want the "icon pack" search traffic, you'd need to build a real APK icon pack later (a separate project).
2. **Icon count: done.** Raised to 100 icons per tone (200 PNGs) on 12 Oct, in line with "100+ icons" packs.
3. **No brand logos.** The icons are generic Material Symbols (Apache 2.0), not Instagram, WhatsApp and so on. That keeps the listing safe from Etsy IP takedowns. Keep it that way.
4. **Mockups:** first photo = before/after phone side by side. Second = icon grid in both tones. Third = "what's inside" graphic. Fourth = one guide page.

### FAQ (Etsy FAQ section, or the end of the description)

**Will this change my real app icons?**
On iPhone, no. Apple doesn't allow that. You create shortcut icons that open your apps, and the guide walks you through it. Your apps are never changed or deleted.

**How long does setup take?**
Calm mode takes about 10 minutes. Full custom icons take 30 to 45 minutes for a main page of 8 to 12 apps.

**Do I need to install anything?**
On iPhone, no. Shortcuts is built in. For custom icons on Android you'll need a free shortcut-maker app, or a launcher that supports custom icon images.

**Is this an Android icon pack for Nova, Niagara or Lawnchair?**
No. The icons are PNG image files, not an installable icon-pack app. Nova and some other launchers let you set an image per app. For Niagara or Lawnchair, use the shortcut-app method in the guide.

**Will notification badges still show?**
Not on shortcut-made icons. Many people find that's the point.

**Will it help with my ADHD or screen time?**
It's a calmer phone setup, not a treatment. Many people find fewer colours and fewer notifications make their phone easier to put down. Results vary from person to person.

**Can I go back to normal?**
Yes, in a couple of minutes. The guide includes a reset section.

**Which apps are covered?**
100 everyday functions, including phone, messages, mail, camera, photos, browser, maps, music, podcasts, notes, calendar, clock, weather, settings, banking, health, fitness, books, reminders, files, video, games, social, food, taxi, travel and more. Choose whichever icon fits each app.
