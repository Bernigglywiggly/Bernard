- **Per 1%, KDP is the only line that earns real £ inside 12 months.** But the cap stops it at 2 paperbacks + 2 hardcovers a week, which is about 3-6% of the allowance.

- **So KDP gets exactly its cap, spent on quality** (hit rate beats count: one title at BSR #1,000 earns ~£5,600 a month; 60 average ones earn ~£456).

- **YouTube earns £0 per 1% today.** It buys subscribers (about 4 per Curve Short at 0.84 per 1,000 views) and the chance of a breakout format.

## 2. What a subscriber costs us, and the deadline

| Measure | Now | With the playbook's end line (2.0 per 1,000) [EST] |

- **The cheapest real lever is conversion** (playbook rule 5 and test T3), not more Shorts.
