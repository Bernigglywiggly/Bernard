# v6 playbook: calm, clean, satisfying

The rules the mockup follows, so every video after it can too.

## 1. Voice and script

The personality of the first full A01: calm, clear and warm, with no jokes. The pull comes from
**perspective**, not from bits.

**The shape of every video**
1. **0–10 s: the event and the number.** No preamble ("hey guys" is the steepest drop-off point;
   [River](https://rivereditor.com/guides/how-to-script-youtube-videos-2026)).
2. **10–30 s: the reframe.** "Sounds small. It isn't." Then the first perspective.
3. **The perspective ladder.** Translate the same number for 3–5 different people: student, gamer,
   business owner, "your time is money", parent, driver, nurse. Each rung is *who → before → after →
   what it means for them*. People only lean in when it's about them, and this makes everyone
   "them" at least once.
4. **What's in it for you**, as chapters: what it does for you, what it costs, how people earn with it,
   what's coming.
5. **An open loop every 2–3 minutes** ("in a minute: the use that pays for itself"), and **a
   pattern change every 60–90 s**: dark to light, card to chart, ASCII to chrome
   ([OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-script-writing/)).
6. **End hopeful and practical:** one thing to try today, one thing to watch for.

**Every number** gets an exact source, 2+ everyday translations, and "what it means for your
money or time". Money and time are what people care about most, so make the benefit explicit.

## 2. Look: Chrome & Marl

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Light marl | `#E7E8EA` + heathered fleck | explaining, cards, chapters |
| Graphite marl | `#14161A` + heathered fleck | forming, revealing, the Tron moments |
| Ink | `#1C1F24` / soft `#6B717C` | type and line work on light |
| Turquoise | `#12B8AC` (glow `#3FE6D8`) | the one accent: what's active, what's new |
| Emerald | `#12A36E` | gains only (+14.1) |
| Chrome | white → steel → a thin dark horizon → faint turquoise reflection | hero numbers only |

**Type:** Michroma (wide, geometric) for numbers and titles; Inter Tight for sentences; IBM Plex Mono
for labels and ASCII.

**Motion rules**
- The camera barely moves (under 2% drift). **The elements move.**
- Every change is a transformation, not a cut: lines *form* things (Tron-style), then *flow* into
  the next form (cores become bars, bars become "14"). Characters *resolve* out of noise (ASCII).
  Cards *dock*.
- Easing is always smooth in-out; nothing snaps unless it means something.
- One "whoa" per chapter at most (the push through the 0, kept gentle).

**Motifs that recur (the brand is in the repetition)**
- **Line formation:** edges light up outward from a seed point, with a bright tip (homage to the
  Tron: Legacy formation work, built with custom line tools; [GMUNK](https://gmunk.com/TRON-Legacy)).
- **The 10×10 "per hundred" grid:** every percentage becomes cells filling in or emptying out.
- **ASCII:** labels decode from characters; big moments dissolve into characters and re-form.
- **Cards and tiles:** editorial, Pinterest-clean, generous margins, one idea each.
- **Technical line work:** dimension lines, ticks, axes, the "+14.1" bracket.

## 3. Sound: layered, restrained, satisfying

- **One quiet sound per visual action, from one family** (research consensus: restraint sounds
  more professional; [Tareno](https://tareno.co/resources/blog/sound-design-for-reels-how-to-use-asmr-and-sound-effects),
  [Envato](https://elements.envato.com/learn/ui-sound-design-ai)):
  - tick when a character resolves
  - pip when a grid cell fills, climbing a pentatonic scale (the "progress" feeling that makes people keep watching)
  - form chirp when a line forms
  - glass chime on completion
  - soft thock when a card docks
  - airy morph as the lines flow into a new form
  - a sub "thum" for the one big line
- **Sync is the satisfaction:** sound lands on the exact frame of the motion.
- **The bed changes key with the world:** Am9 in the dark, Cmaj9 when the light opens on "It isn't".
- **Levels:** the mix sits at -14 LUFS, with the SFX 10–20 dB under the voice.

## 4. Techniques to keep using (underused on YouTube)

Morph transitions between metaphors · match cuts on shape (a bar outline becomes a digit) ·
ASCII decode/resolve · line formations · data that fills (grids, counters) · 2D cards tilting into 3D ·
"same number, different meaning" collapses (many views merging into one)
([School of Motion](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/match-cuts)).

## 5. Shorts from the same file

Every perspective card is a 20–30 s Short. Hook: "+14 on a coding test. Here's what that means if
you're at uni." Loop it: end on the chrome "+14" that opens it
([looping structure](https://virvid.ai/blog/looping-structure-shorts-retention-2026)).

## 6. The high-budget checklist

A consistent grid and margins · 3 type sizes max · crisp vector at 1080p/4K (no upscaling) ·
textured grounds (marl), never flat digital grey · soft, real shadows · eased motion ·
sound on every motion · no stock footage · every number sourced on screen.

## 7. Board motifs (v6.1, from the CDE board)

Each one is used once and only where it explains something; each has its own sound.

| Motif | Where (hook) | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Perspective ground grid + projection lines | 0–6 s, the two cores | scan |
| HUD brackets, crosshairs, callouts | 0–11 s, labels and pins | blip |
| Ridgeline terrain with pins (52.3 → 66.4) | 6–11 s, the scores as a landscape | scan, count |
| Spectral glint lines on chrome | 12.8 s, the "+14" | glint, shimmer |
| Particle ring | 14.3 s, the one big line | burst, thum |
| Halftone dot-matrix icons | 15–41 s, the perspective cards | dots, thock |
| Exploded isometric stack, pierced by one turquoise line | 41.9–43.6 s, "same number, every layer" | lift, pierce |
| Concentric ring tunnel | 52.6 s, the push through the "0" | zoom, tunnel |

Left out on purpose (didn't fit the calm look): maps (saved for A02), glass hands, gradients.
