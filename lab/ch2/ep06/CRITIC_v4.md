## 4. Verdict

Not yet, but close, and for the first time the list of picture faults is shorter than the list of things that work.
v4 really did remove the black frame, the dim ghost labels, the note on the grid, the cell jammed in the left edge,
the cut café node and the tick marks over the meter label, and the last chapter's argument is now a size a person can
read. Two claims are true as written (e, f); the other four are half true, and the pattern is the same as before: a
fix that is real at one edge of the frame opens a fault at another. The last chapter no longer loses a node on the
left, but it now scrolls its own headline quote and its first line through the top edge for the final 18 seconds
(X1), which is the one picture fault I would not publish with, and it is a ten-minute fix because the frame has the
spare room. The meters scene has its tracks, but they are hairlines and 7 px labels in a frame that does not change
for eight seconds, and there are four of them under a label that says three (X2). The transits no longer look broken;
they look rushed: chopped glyphs for about 0.4 s each, a headline sliced by the left edge on the way in and out, an
overview that never stops moving, and a thumbnail arrival in the dark. Hold the last chapter on one framing, fix the
three-versus-four meters and brighten the empty tracks, hold or drop the overview, and the picture is publishable
with the remaining items as known compromises. The spoken interchange claims (item 4) remain a hard stop on their own.
