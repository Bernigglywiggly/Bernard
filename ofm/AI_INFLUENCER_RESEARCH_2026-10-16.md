**Uncomfortable version**
- No openly-AI woman in her late 30s is visibly winning on Instagram today. The lane is open, and it is also unproven.
- The accounts that grow fastest are comedy characters. Most of them don't sell subscriptions. The accounts that sell subscriptions either hide that they are AI (and get banned), or get age-gated.
- Nadia has to be a character people quote, not a face they scroll past. Rank 1 is the change that decides whether the day-14 reach gate passes.
