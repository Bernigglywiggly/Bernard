## What the numbers say
1. **The first second is the leak.** Only 42-60% of Curve views become engaged views (the rest swipe away almost at
   once), but the people who stay watch 80-84% and loop. Raising the engaged share is the biggest lever we have.
2. **Search matters for Shorts too:** 9% of views come from YouTube search, so titles that are the exact question
   people type keep paying after the feed moves on.
3. **The audience is American** (52% US, plus Canada and Australia): post for US daytime, and US-priced RPM applies
   once monetised.
4. **Subscribers lag views:** about 0.8 subscribers per 1,000 views and almost no shares (9 on 15k views). A series
   and a reason to follow are missing.
5. **Only one channel works.** The other three have 302 views between them in 22 videos.
