### Gumroad and the other stores (row 4)

### Affiliates (row 5)
- ElevenLabs: 22% for the first 12 months of a paying customer (not enterprise), 90-day cookie, paid via PartnerStack once the referred plan has been active 90 days, $5 minimum. **P** (ElevenLabs' own pages)
