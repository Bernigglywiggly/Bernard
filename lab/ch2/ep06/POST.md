## Description
Every time a card is tapped, a message crosses Visa's network, and the money moves between two banks. Visa lends none
of it. This film follows one payment (a café in Lisbon, a bank in Ohio: an illustration, not a real transaction)
to show what Visa charges, what it hands back, and why about half of its net revenue ends the year as profit.

Figures are for Visa's fiscal 2025 (year to 30 September 2025).
