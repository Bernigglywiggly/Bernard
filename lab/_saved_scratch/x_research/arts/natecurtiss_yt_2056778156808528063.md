Thousands of channels are getting demonetized right now, and YouTube explained why.

In July 2025 YouTube quietly renamed its "repetitious content" policy to "inauthentic content." That rename is not cosmetic. It is a signal of exactly what they are going after.

Inauthentic content means: mass-produced videos that follow the same template. Channels that upload dozens of videos a week with no variation. Slideshows with AI narration and no real human input. Faceless channels where every video is interchangeable. Content that looks like it was made by a bot because it was.

YouTube is not banning AI. They are banning the absence of a creator.

The rule is simple. If a viewer cannot tell that a real person made this, with a real point of view, real judgment, and real intention, YouTube does not want to monetize it. Not because the content is necessarily bad. Because it is replaceable. And YouTube does not pay for replaceable.

A 588,000 subscriber channel earning $30,000 a month in ad revenue was fully demonetized in early 2026. Still getting close to a million views a month. The reach did not disappear. The monetization did. Because the content was templated and interchangeable.

The channels surviving this are the ones that look human. Real voice. Real editing decisions. Real variation between videos. Real authorship.

AI as a tool is fine. AI as the creator is what gets you banned.

Build something that could only come from you. That is the only thing YouTube cannot flag.
