## Description (draft)
Tap the yellow button and spend $100. Last year PayPal kept about $1.85 of every $100 that moved through it, a little
over half of that went straight out again to card networks, banks and losses, and about 34 cents ended up as profit.
This film follows one payment all the way through: what the shop pays, where the money goes, the $1.3 billion PayPal
earned from money people leave sitting in their balances, and why the company says its famous button is where it's
struggling.
