# Six Floors: the episode format (v7)

Every episode is one elevator ride: down through six floors and back up. A **depth gauge** on screen shows
the floor, so viewers feel themselves going deeper (our answer to pollar's timeline spine). One story, one
number, one hidden rule, one big idea, one what-if, one thing to do tonight.

| Floor | Job | Sounds like | Looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 · GROUND** | The event, stated flat. A present-state hook, then a flashback with a number. | "This task takes a human expert sixteen hours." | editorial fact cards, the metric's ruler appears |
| **1 · MECHANISM** | How it actually works underneath. The hidden rule. | "Here's how you measure that." | 3D data the camera glides over, a curve drawing, the rule highlighted |
| **2 · YOU** | The same number for several people (the v6 perspective ladder). Time and money. | "Sixteen hours is two working days." | tiles and cards dock, the metric in human units |
| **3 · IDEA** | The principle that explains a whole class of things (science, economics, mind). | "We think in steps. Growth like this thinks in folds." | one hero visual: the concept made physical |
| **4 · IMAGINE** | A what-if that extends the logic, **labelled as imagination**. Stretch the mind; end on a question. | "So imagine it keeps going. Not a forecast. A what-if." | the drop (silence → Shepard fall → sub), then the dream layer: ASCII, point clouds, ghost cards, dotted "if" markers |
| **5 · SURFACE** | One thing to do tonight, then a mirrored close. | "It took six years to get from two seconds to an hour. It took one more to get from an hour to sixteen." | the gauge climbs back up, two mirrored lines, sources |

## Rules
- **Rigour first (pollar's gift):** every number carries a date and a source on screen; quotes get a highlighter;
  sources roll at the end. Imagination is always flagged (IMAGINE on the gauge, dotted markers, "if the trend held").
- **One persistent metric ruler** per episode (days, dollars, hours, FLOPs), gaining a marker at every beat.
- **Silence is punctuation:** a 0.2–0.4 s full drop before every reveal (measured in the inspo). The biggest drop
  is the fall into IMAGINE, with the vortex sound.
- **Voice:** calm, declarative, ~140–150 wpm, fragments for the big beats. No hype words, no "guys".
- **Motion:** calm and orchestrated. The camera only moves when it reveals something; the IMAGINE floor is where it may fly.
- **Close symmetric:** the last two lines mirror each other (numbers, lengths, rhythm).
- **Length:** 2:00–2:30 vertical (TikTok and Shorts both take up to 3 minutes), with a 16:9 cut for YouTube long-form.

## The IMAGINE toolkit (ten moves for floor 4)
Each move takes the episode's number or mechanism and pushes it somewhere the mind hasn't been. Always label it,
always end on a question, and keep one foot in the facts (say what would have to be true).

| Move | How it works | Example (AI) |
|---|---|---|
| **Extend the curve** | "If the trend held…", with dotted markers | a month of work handed over by mid-2027 (EP01) |
| **Price → zero** | Imagine the scarce thing is free | a genius for 8p an hour: what happens to tutoring, law, medicine? |
| **Scale shift** | Blow it up or shrink it down | a data centre the size of a city; a model on a grain of rice |
| **Time jump** | 10, 100 or 1,000 years on, looking back | a 2126 history lesson about "the year thinking got cheap" |
| **Swap the viewpoint** | Tell it from the thing's side | a day in the life of a token; the chip that talks to you (the pilot) |
| **Remove a constraint** | No sleep, no forgetting, no distance | a colleague who never forgets a conversation: blessing or curse? |
| **Invert it** | The opposite world | AI that only asks questions and never answers |
| **Old thought experiment, new clothes** | Borges' library, Laplace's demon, the Ship of Theseus, the Chinese room, the Fermi paradox | if every part of your job is replaced one by one, is it still your job? |
| **The last one** | The last human doing X | the last person who ever had to fill in a form |
| **Hand it to you** | Put the viewer in charge | you get a thousand tireless interns tomorrow: first three tasks? |

Pair every IMAGINE with the drop (silence → Shepard fall → sub) and the dream layer, and bring the viewer back
up with one practical step, so the fantasy converts into action.
