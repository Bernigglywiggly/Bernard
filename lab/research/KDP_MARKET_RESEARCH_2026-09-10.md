## 3. THE CATEGORY ERROR — probably the cheapest fix available
His book sits in `Books › Humor & Entertainment › Humor › Law & Crime`.
Every seller above ranks in `Word Search Games`. **Puzzle buyers never browse Humor.**
Category can be changed on a live listing in minutes via KDP support. Doing this across all
~20 existing titles costs nothing and is the only zero-effort lever on the table.
It will not rescue a dead niche, but it is free and it is currently guaranteed-zero.

## 4. SEASONALITY IS REAL AND MEASURABLE
Cozy Autumn is #841 **right now, in September**, because it is autumn. The same publisher's
Cozy Christmas sits at #37,479 today and will climb hard through Nov–Dec. Nostalgic
Christmas (published Nov 2025) has fallen to #294,962 in the off-season.
Seasonal titles must be live **8–10 weeks before** the season to accumulate rank and reviews.
For Christmas 2026 that window is closing this month.

## 6. RECOMMENDATION

**Do NOT make another 20 true crime books.** The data says the ceiling is under 1 sale/day
even executed perfectly. Twenty more is twenty times zero.

**Publish the 15 already built.** They are finished, they now pass the KDP gate, and
shipping them is free. Expect little; do it anyway because the cost is zero.

**Fix the categories on the existing ~20.** Free, fast, and they are currently invisible.

**If continuing on KDP, the only formula the data supports:**
- theme = a mood/identity/season, never a subject-trivia topic
- ~100 puzzles, 105+ pages
- price £10.49–£10.99
- categories: Word Search Games + Word Games + Puzzles
- large print, aimed at adults and seniors
- live 8–10 weeks before its season

**The honest caveat.** The winners hold 252–516 reviews. Starting from 0 against them is
hard and slow, and nothing here reaches £2,000/month this year. KDP low-content is a
compounding asset with a long fuse, not a fast income. Judge it on that basis or not at all.
