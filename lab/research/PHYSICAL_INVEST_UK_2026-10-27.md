## 2. Detail

### Row 1: Rent a Room
- Up to **£7,500 a year tax-free** from letting furnished accommodation in your own home; halved to £3,750 if the income is shared. Below the threshold it's automatic; above it, fill in a tax return and opt in. **P (gov.uk)**
- Councils say tenants as well as owners can use it if they live there (resident landlord); not for homes split into separate flats. **P (council pages)**
- Short stays via Airbnb in your own home are covered according to a tax-software vendor; gov.uk didn't confirm it in the extract. **D**
- If he rents: his tenancy probably needs the landlord's written OK; if he owns: his mortgage lender's. **[K]**

### Row 2: parking
- JustPark takes 15%; public liability insurance about £80 a year (UK Tax Calculators). Earnings examples are JustPark's own: up to £180 a month near the O2 and Emirates, £75-105 near Anfield and Murrayfield; "top hosts over £4,000 a year". London average about £13 a day (Leasing Options, 2025 data). **D/C**
- The £1,000 property allowance covers the first £1,000 a year. **C**
- Away from a station, venue or hospital, expect little: **E**

### Row 3: co-hosting
- UK co-host commission 10-25% of gross bookings; messaging and calendar only sits at 10-15% (Houst blog). Airbnb's own host fee moved to a single 15.5% for most UK hosts on 22 Jun 2026 (two blogs; check Airbnb's page). **D**

### Rows 4 and 8: storage
- **Pods:** Store First sold investors self-storage pods promising 8% a year rising to 12%; it raised over £209 million from about 6,600 investors (2011-2016). The government sought a winding-up order in 2017; investors reportedly got no returns and were left liable for business rates until allowed to surrender the pods. The FCA shut its sister scheme Park First in 2017, alleging an illegal collective investment scheme. **C (Bondreview, court filings via press; advocacy-leaning)**
- **Auctions:** facilities sell a defaulted tenant's whole unit to recover unpaid rent, mostly through online platforms in the UK; lots are as seen, must be paid for and cleared fast; surplus usually goes back to the tenant. No UK data on buyers' resale profits was found. **D**

### Row 5: vending
- New snack and drink machines £2,000-6,000; coffee £3,000-15,000; typical net margin 20-25%; cold drinks ~40% of UK vending sales (UK guides). One case study: a £700 used machine grew to 27 machines and £7,000+ a month gross, a single success story. **D**
- Location drives revenue; template margins are optimistic. **D**

### Row 6: rent-to-rent and short lets
- Lawful only with the right contracts, the owner's lender consent, licensing and insurance. Under the Renters' Rights Act (from 1 May 2026) owners lose a clear right to vacant possession when the deal ends and carry more liability for the operator's breaches. **C**
- London caps short lets at 90 nights a year without planning permission; Scotland requires a licence; Article 4 areas need permission. England's national short-let register is due to go live in **March 2027** (Culture Secretary, 3 Sep 2026); listings will need a registration number. **C**
- The furnished holiday lettings tax regime was abolished from 6 April 2025. **[K]**
- Penalties include rent repayment orders of up to 12 months' rent for serious HMO breaches. **C**

### Row 7: deal sourcing
- Sourcing for a client counts as estate agency work: HMRC anti-money-laundering supervision, a government-approved redress scheme, ICO registration, professional indemnity insurance, client-money protection if holding money. HMRC fined one unregistered sourcer £1,700. The Property Redress Scheme expects fees to be refunded if a deal doesn't complete. **C**
