Method: web search extracts of the ICO's guidance and law-firm summaries; pages not opened. Not legal advice: the live ICO

## The short version

**A cold Facebook or Instagram DM (or email or text) selling the Reels pack to a sole trader or an ordinary partnership needs
their consent first.** The ICO counts social-media direct messages as "electronic mail", and most independent takeaways are
sole traders or partnerships. Only 1 of the 52 trading names on the send sheet shows "Ltd" (Bear Coffee Company Ltd).
Walk-ins, letters and screened phone calls are fine.

## The rules, by route

| Route | Sole trader / ordinary partnership | Limited company / LLP | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook / Instagram DM, email, SMS | **Needs consent** (or a "soft opt-in" from a previous sale). Contact details found publicly are not consent | Allowed; say who's sending and give an easy opt-out | ICO, "Electronic mail marketing" and "Business-to-business marketing" |
| Live phone call | Allowed unless the number is on the **TPS**: screen first | Allowed unless on the **CTPS**: screen first | Bird & Bird ICO enforcement summaries; ICO fines for unscreened business calls (Ice Communications £80,000; UK Direct Business Solutions £100,000) |
| Automated / recorded calls | Need consent | Need consent | [K] PECR reg. 19 |
| Walking in and showing the reel on a phone | No PECR issue | No PECR issue | PECR covers electronic communications only [K] |
| A posted card with a QR code to their own reel | No PECR issue (UK GDPR applies if addressed to a named person) | Same | [K] |

the reel is personalised, so showing it in person is the strongest version of the pitch.
