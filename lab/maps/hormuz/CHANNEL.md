## One-line promise
**Every week, one place on the map that quietly controls something you pay for, explained with the real numbers and a verdict.**

## The recurring look
- **Typographic maps, not satellite imagery**: black ground, coastlines as thin lines, places set as type with a mono
  sub-line (name, then "26.6N 56.4E · 33 KM" style coordinates and one figure).
- **Nested zooms** in every film: the narrow place → the region → the world → back to the same narrow place for the
  last line (the loop that ties open and close together).
- **Glowing routes drawn on over the beat** (shipping lanes, pipelines, cables, rail), and **hatched zones** for
  lanes, claims and blockade areas. Pins for events, always labelled with date and source.
- **Every schematic is labelled as schematic** in the caption line ("LANES DRAWN SCHEMATICALLY"); every map has a
  source line. That honesty is part of the brand and protects against "AI slop" comments.
- Number cards, split cards and quotes from the house engine fill the beats the map does not carry (about 60/40
  cards to maps; this film is 38% maps).
- To differ from the other channels on the same engine (CRAFT Learned 9 Oct, point 3): this channel should get its
  own palette (e.g. sea-chart amber/teal on near-black rather than The Curve's cyan) and drop the numbered rail in
  favour of a coordinate readout in the corner. Decide before launch.
