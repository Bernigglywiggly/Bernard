## 7. Top five constraints

1. Bio says AI, and no message ever claims a real person. [Fanvue](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538752-what-is-an-ai-generated-model)
2. He passes ID + selfie himself; nobody can do it for him. [Fanvue](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9539091-passing-kyc-and-creating-multiple-accounts-as-an-ai-creator)
3. Invented face only, clearly adult. No real-person likeness, no youthful look. [Fanvue guidelines](https://legal.fanvue.com/community-guidelines) and [UK offence](https://api.parliament.uk/committees/publications/51220)
4. Instagram: AI-generated profile label on, all posts SFW, or reach is cut. [Instagram](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/ai-generated-profile-label)
5. No off-platform payments, no moving chats off Fanvue, no unapproved automation. [Fanvue](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/12179988-how-to-stay-compliant-on-fanvue)
