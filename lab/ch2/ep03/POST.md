## Description

At Costco, a hot dog and a drink still cost $1.50, the same price since 1985. A whole roast chicken costs $4.99. And in the year to August 2026, Costco made $9.2 billion of profit. The shopping isn't really what it sells. The card in your wallet is.

In fiscal 2026 Costco collected $5.9 billion in membership fees: about half of its $11.7 billion operating profit. Take the fees away and $297 billion of goods earned about two cents on every dollar. This is how the warehouse model works (Sol Price's Price Club, the founders' markup limits, fewer than 4,000 products), why more than nine in ten members renew even after a fee rise, and how to tell whether the card, or the Executive upgrade, actually pays for you.
