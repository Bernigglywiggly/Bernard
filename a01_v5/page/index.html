Five visual palettes, each one a room on the same amber drift line. The room changes with what the voice is doing: facts, street translation, jokes, the deep line. The line, the type and the finish never change, so the variety comes across as range instead of randomness.

What never changes

The five constants

These travel through every world. They are why a dithered joke burst and a chrome hero shot still look like the same channel.

The amber line#FFB23F, in every shot, always the warmest thing in frame. Even the 3-colour dither keeps amber as one of its colours.
Near-black edges#07080B. Every world falls back to the same ground at its edges.
Two typefacesUnbounded for numbers and headlines, IBM Plex Mono for labels. No third face, ever.
One finishThe same highlight bloom, vignette and film grain on every shot, whatever engine or world it came from.
The frameCrop marks, A01, the world code, a mini drift line showing where you are, and a swatch whose last square is always amber.

On your Mac

Render it properly

  1. Pull the scripts from a01_v5/ on the claude/funny-newton-gd9w8v branch of your Bernard repo.
  2. Run python3 posters.py, then each shot through Blender at --res 1920x1080 --samples 24, then python3 assemble.py. The README has the exact loop.
  3. Check the S06 numbers (52.3 and 66.4) against the A01 script before anything goes public.
  4. If you re-record the Cousin in your own voice, re-time the cut points in shots.py; every animation cue follows them.
