| # | Sum | Result |

| 90 | 30 µm glass at 1.4 mm radius: 0.030 / (2 x 1.4) | **1.1% stretch**, right at glass's ~1% cracking range (row 71). Why it must be toughened (rows 75, 79) |
| 91 | Normal 0.5 mm phone glass at the same fold: 0.5 / 2.8 | **18% stretch**, about 17x more. It would shatter |
| 92 | Same 30 µm glass at 2 mm: 0.75%. At 1 mm: 1.5% | Every 0.1 mm of radius matters |
| 93 | "Book pages", illustrative stack, NOT Duo numbers: 10 layers x 30 µm = 0.3 mm. Glued solid at 1.4 mm: 0.3 / 2.8 | **10.7%**. Free to slide: each layer 0.03 / 2.8 = **1.1%**. Sliding cuts the peak stretch 10x |
| 94 | Duo inference: a 180° loop inside HTX's ~3.85 mm teardrop must curve at 3.85 / 2 = 1.9 mm radius or tighter somewhere | A 30 µm layer there would stretch 0.78% or more. Inference only: Apple publishes neither number |

### The chain, for the diagram (rows in brackets)

1. Fold the phone: each layer's outer face stretches, its inner face squeezes (70, 72).
2. Glass: stretched, plus a tiny flaw, so a crack can run. Thin glass keeps the stretch near 1% (71, 75, 90, 91).
3. Plastic top and glue: they bend without cracking, but don't fully spring back, so the crease deepens with use (81-83, 60, 61).
4. Plastic is soft, so it scratches and dents: Mohs 2 and fingernail grooves in J1 (45, 47, 85).
5. Apple's fix: thin layers that slide like book pages, glass both sides, a titanium plate, matte to hide the crease (20-26, 93).
6. JRE's bend: the phone flexed further than he expected and the screen broke (54-56). Cause unknown: don't guess on screen.

## 9. Do NOT claim

- That Apple publishes a fold count, glass thickness, fold radius or crease depth (row 30-31). Row 94 is an inference, label it.
- That Samsung makes the panel or glass (row 33): reported only.
- "10 layers" exactly: Apple says "more than 10".
- That JRE disproved IP68 (rows 11, 65): the dust jammed the hinge; he says it didn't get in through the screen or frame.
- That JRE's green line runs along the fold, or why it is green, or what caused it (rows 56-57).
- A widespread defect or "Crackgate" as fact (row 63): anecdotes, no failure rate.
- That Apple gave a cause, a number of affected units, or a recall: it said "a small number", AppleCare replacements, no cause (row 34). Never use owner crack photos (row 34b).
- Repair prices as Apple's ($49, $999, $1,499): reported, not seen on Apple's page (rows 48-49).
- His other spec lines (no telephoto, 12 MP / 4 MP selfie cameras, 35 W, 25 W): unchecked; Apple says the main camera
  gives an "optical-quality 2x Telephoto" crop (A1), so "no telephoto" is at least arguable.
- "First folding phone with dual ultra-thin glass" (row 40): his claim.
- That the Stern test equals "a year of use" (P5's estimate) or that the Galaxy Z Fold8 passed the same test (forum comment).
- Anything from his case segment (13:00-14:45): sponsor content (dbrand).
