## Title
**The Original Ponzi Scheme: 50% in 45 Days, and the Newspaper That Did the Maths**

## Description
Boston, July 1920. A crowd fills School Street, and the man who has their money walks through it handing out coffee and hot dogs. Charles Ponzi promised 50% profit in 45 days, paid his first investors in full, and turned every happy customer into a salesman, until a newspaper asked one question: where is the money coming from?

This is the story of the man who gave the scam its name: the postal coupon behind the pitch, the arithmetic that sank it, the run he survived, the past that caught up with him, and why the same trick is still running today.
