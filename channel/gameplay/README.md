# Gameplay rants (PS5, the user's own channel)
The user records and rants; anonymous (no face; a Brand Account so the channel name shows, not theirs).
- Capture: PS5 Settings > System > HDMI > HDCP off; a capture card (e.g. Elgato HD60 X) into OBS; the mic on its own track.
- Mic: a dynamic mic (Fifine AM8 ~GBP 50, Rode PodMic USB ~GBP 180, Shure MV7+ ~GBP 250), 5-10 cm from the mouth.
- Voice changer: real-time ones sound robotic on laughs and shouts; best is recording clean and converting after
  (ElevenLabs Voice Changer, paid per minute; or RVC/Applio locally, free with an NVIDIA GPU). The real voice with no
  face is anonymous enough for most people.
- Monetisation needs real commentary on the gameplay; in-game music can trigger copyright claims.
