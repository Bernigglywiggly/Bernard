# Five channels: the two rules that shape the plan, and the order to build them

Written 1 Oct 2026 (overnight cloud session). Goal: five channels at about $1,000 a month each.

## Rule 1: the monetisation bar doubles on 1 Feb 2027
- **Until 31 Jan 2027:** 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months, or 10M Shorts views in 90 days.
- **From 1 Feb 2027 (new applicants):** 1,000 subscribers + **8,000** watch hours in 365 days, or **20M** Shorts views in
  90 days. Channels already in the Partner Programme keep their place. Shorts ad revenue also needs 10M Shorts views per
  90 days to keep earning. Announced Aug 2026.
  Sources: [Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/08/youtube-doubles-partner-program-monetization-requirements-2027-update.html),
  [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/technology-tech-news-youtube-partner-program-monetisation-rules-2027-watch-hours-shorts-views-126081100708_1.html),
  [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/).
- **What it means:** The Curve has four months to get in under the old bar: about 33 watch hours a day, or 10M Shorts
  views in a 90-day window. Long-form (Season One, 13 min; future films 8+ min) earns the hours; daily Shorts are the
  other route. Every channel started later should plan for the doubled bar.

## Rule 2: "inauthentic content" (templated, mass-produced AI) gets channels removed
- Renamed from "repetitious content" in July 2025; enforced per channel since a January 2026 wave. Strikes: a warning,
  a 90-day monetisation suspension, then removal from the Partner Programme.
- **Allowed:** an AI narrator with disclosed synthetic content, original research and structure, AI visuals serving
  real teaching. **Targeted:** templates with small changes, the same voice over slideshows, copies across channels.
  Source: [LensPOV summary](https://lenspov.com/articles/youtube-ai-content-demonetization-2026).
- **What it means:** five channels cannot be one template re-skinned five times. Each needs its own format, look,
  narrator, music and research angle. The Curve already does the right things (sourced figures on screen, an original
  engine, labelled what-ifs, disclosure ticked on every upload).

## The money, honestly
- Survey ranges for 2026 (US-heavy audiences; a new channel starts lower): business/finance $10-28 RPM, AI tools and
  B2B software $13-45, education $8-18 ([OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-niches-guide/),
  [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/blog/youtube-rpm-by-niche-2026)). Treat the top of each range as optimistic.
- At $10 RPM, $1,000 a month is **100,000 monetised long-form views a month** per channel (about 3,300 a day).
- Shorts pay a small fraction of that: use them to bring people to the long films and to reach the Shorts route.

## Order
1. **Now:** The Curve posts a film every two days and shorts daily; Season One goes up the day after the fifth film.
2. **Weeks 2-3:** build Channel 2's first three films and their shorts before launch, with its own palette, its own
   narrator (an ElevenLabs designed voice, not George) and its own music bed.
3. **Channels 3-5** only after The Curve or Channel 2 shows traction (click-through above ~4%, average view duration above
   ~40%), so the factory copies what works rather than what doesn't.
