## 3. FCA: what can never go on our channels

| Rule | Detail | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Promoting a credit card, loan, BNPL, investment, trading or crypto product is a **financial promotion** | Only an FCA-authorised firm can make one, or approve someone else's. A breach is a **criminal offence: up to 2 years in prison, an unlimited fine, or both** | C (law-firm summaries of FCA FG24/1) |
| FG24/1 (FCA, 26 Mar 2024) covers influencers and affiliate marketers | It explains when their posts fall inside the rules; it adds no new rules, it applies the existing ones to social media | C |
| Introducing people to lenders for a fee is **credit broking**, a regulated activity | Affiliates do it as an authorised firm's appointed representative or "introducer appointed representative" | P (FCA's credit-broker guide) |
| Enforcement is real | FCA charged 9 finfluencers on 16 May 2024 (an FX scheme on Instagram); one source reports seven sentenced in Feb 2026 (unverified); April 2026 "week of action" with 17 regulators | C/D |

**Our rule:** no card, loan, bank-bonus, trading or crypto links or "how to earn points" content on The Curve, Money Crimes, How They Profit, the AI persona or any Short, ever. Explaining a scam (Money Crimes) is fine; recommending a product is not. Using cards and switch bonuses himself is fine: personal use isn't a promotion. **[K] on the personal-use line**
