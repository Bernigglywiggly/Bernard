## Description

Denmark is building the world's longest immersed tunnel: 18 km under the Baltic to Germany, made of 89 concrete
elements that each weigh more than 73,500 tonnes. The first went down in May 2026. By 28 September 2026, four were
in place. The 2029 opening has been dropped and no new date has been published. This film explains how you sink a
tunnel, what it costs, why it is late, and what the pace so far implies.

Figures are as published on the dates shown. The "8 to 11 years at this pace" sum is our own arithmetic, not a forecast.
Illustrations are generated diagrams, not photographs. Recorded 9 October 2026.
