## Description

Boston, July 1920. A line of people four abreast stretches down School Street, and the man who has their money walks along it handing out coffee and doughnuts. Charles Ponzi promised 50% profit in 45 days, paid his first investors in full, and turned every happy customer into a salesman, until a newspaper asked one question: where is the money coming from?

This is the true story of the man who gave the scam its name: the postal coupon behind the pitch, the arithmetic that sank it, the run he survived, the past that caught up with him, and why the same trick is still running today.
