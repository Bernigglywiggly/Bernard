## Do not claim
- An exact border length (sources say 135 m and 150 m); "the shortest border in the world" (unchecked).
- That a true quadripoint exists (it is two tripoints; the positions in the river were never fully ratified).
- The 2003 ferry capsizing (a tragedy hook; not this format).
- "Trucks waited a week" (one source) or "transit time cut 40%" (unverified analyst claim).
- That the railway is in service (Wikipedia: "currently unconnected").
- Any political or military angle (Caprivi history, sanctions): keep it a map curiosity.
