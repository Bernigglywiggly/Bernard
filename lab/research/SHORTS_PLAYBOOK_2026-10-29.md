# Shorts playbook 2026: fix the first-second leak, raise subs per 1,000 views (Thu 29 Oct 2026)

Method: web research read 30 Oct 2026 (YouTube Help and blog, Creator Insider via press, vendor datasets) on top of our 15-28 Oct analytics. Grades A-D as briefed; P = our analytics; [EST] = estimate with its assumption; [S#] = sources at the end.

| 2 | **End: spoken promise and the word "subscribe", then the loop frame** | Subs per 1,000 | C [S15] · A [S10] | 0.8 → 1.5-2.5 [EST: a fifth of one long-form test's 13x lift] |

## 3. First second: the recipe for our engine

| Time | Picture | Text | Sound | Why (grade) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frame 0 | The clip's most impossible-looking instant, already moving, cropped tall, subject filling the frame; one drawn circle or arrow on the detail | A question of 5 words or fewer, top third, ≥ 68 px ("WHY DIDN'T IT FALL?") | The clip's own sound | Swipe decides reach (A [S3], P); text overlays hook 1.4x more (B, TikTok [S23]); motion grabs gaze (lab, ATTENTION §3) |
| 0-0.5 s | Still moving | Captions from the first word | Voice in, no music lead-in | CRAFT §9 (P) |
| 0-3 s | Clip continues | Line 1: the answer or the stake | Voice | Answer-first ran 37x our median (P) |
| 1-2 s | Our drawing traces the mechanism over the clip | n/a | One crisp SFX | Authorship (A [S5]); a change every 1.5-2.5 s (lab, ATTENTION §1) |
| Never, 0-3 s | Logo, name card, black frame, held still, number card | "In this video" | Music-only intro | P (24 Oct); a 30-50% drop in seconds 1-3 is typical (D [S19]) |

- [ ] Before render → **sound-off test: a stranger thinks "wait, what?" by second 2, or re-cut**

- [ ] Frame-0 text → **the viewer's own question, never a claim or a number**

- [ ] End line → **"Subscribe for one machine in trouble, explained, every day", then the loop frame (T3)**

- [ ] Promise → **the same line on the banner, the trailer and every end frame**

- [ ] Series → **a public playlist now; Shorts series once in YPP**

| Item | Evidence | Grade, source |

| "Part N of 5" | a template pattern | CRAFT §1 · A [S1] |
| Related-video link | one per Short, to a film or Short on our channel; match intent; the film's first 5-10 s must pay off | A [S10] |
| Link lift | +10% first-30-day views on new uploads where Shorts send ≥ 2% of long-form traffic (5,000+ channels) | B [S11] |
| Timing | Shorts within 3 days of a film sent it more views | C [S11] |
| Our long films | 4-126 views, 3-22% viewed | P |

- [ ] Series → **one recurring subject, never one story stretched (RUNBOOK §8b)**

- [ ] Link → **the film on the same object if it opens on that object; else the previous franchise Short**

- [ ] Film launch → **two Shorts cut from each new film, posted in its first days**

- [ ] Comments → **pin one factual question plus the maker's credit; reply in the first hour (D; T7)**

## 9. Inauthentic-content safety (AI voice + own drawings)

| YouTube's position (paraphrased; A [S1] unless marked) | Our risk | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| No pay: templated stories or slideshows with little narrative, commentary or teaching | Low | The explanation is the spine |
| No pay: AI content from generic templates that looks mass-produced; interchangeable videos | Medium: one composition file | Rotate structures |
| Fine: same intro and outro if the rest differs; a distinct storyline per video; AI for unique visuals | n/a | Repeat brand marks, not skeletons |
| Reuse only with a visible difference; not clips strung together, nor readings of others' material | Low-medium | Borrowed footage under half the runtime |
| 1 Oct 2026 ranking: a remix needs its own value, "not just VO descriptions of what's happening on screen" (Rene Ritchie [S5]) | Medium: "the duck rolls over" lines | Every line adds a why, a number or a verdict |
| No pay: AI personas posing as human experts on money, health, law or politics | Real for the money channels | Never present the narrator as a human expert |

- [ ] Each script → **strike lines that only describe the clip**

- [ ] Each week → **no structure three days running (cold open, myth vs fact, scale ladder, X-ray freeze, timeline)**

- [ ] Credit → **name the maker on screen and in the description; credit is not originality [S5]**

## 11. Tests (one change each)

| Test | The only change | Metric | Pass mark | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Frame-0 motion | moving clip vs held still with title | Stayed to watch | +8 points | 5 per arm, alternating days |
| T2 Frame-0 text | question vs claim | Stayed to watch | +5 points | 5 pairs |
| T3 End line | spoken promise + "subscribe" vs pure loop | Subs per 1,000 engaged views | ≥ 2.0, average viewed down ≤ 5 points | 6 per arm |
| T4 Link target | film vs previous Short | Target's views from the Short per 1,000 engaged views | film ≥ Short | 6 per arm |
| T5 Length | 25-30 s vs 45-55 s, same story | Engaged views and subs per 1,000 at 7 days | long wins only if both are higher | 4 pairs |
| T6 B-slot time | 12:00 vs 23:00 UK | Engaged views at 48 h | +20% | 4 weeks each |
| T7 Pinned comment | question pinned vs none | Comments per 1,000 engaged views | 2x | 6 per arm |

Compare each arm with the trailing median of the last 10 Shorts. A winner needs the same direction in most pairs.

Not found, so not claimed: an official stayed-to-watch benchmark; subs per 1,000 by niche; a controlled study of frame-0 motion; ranking weights for loops or shares. Single-source 2026 claims left out until checked: Shorts dislike button removed (Jun 2026), custom Shorts thumbnails (Jul 2026).
