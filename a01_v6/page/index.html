The playbook

The rules this follows

  1. 0–10 s: the event and the number, no preamble. 10–30 s: "sounds small, it isn't", then the first perspective.
  2. A perspective ladder: the same number for 3–5 different people, each ending in their time or money.
  3. Chapters answer: what it does for you, what it costs, how people earn with it, what's coming.
  4. An open loop every 2–3 minutes; a calm pattern change every 60–90 s (dark to light, card to grid to ASCII to chrome).
  5. The camera stays still; the elements transform. One gentle "whoa" per chapter.
  6. One quiet sound per motion, one family, synced to the frame. Repeated events climb a scale.
  7. Every perspective card becomes a looping Short.
