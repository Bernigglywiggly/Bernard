# Promo scripts: Low-Stim Phone Kit

10 short videos for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, 7 to 20 seconds each. All are screen recordings or phone-in-hand shots. No face needed.

**House rules for every script**
- Hook text is on screen by **0.0 s**, large, white on dark, and stays for at least 1.5 s.
- **No results claims.** No "my screen time dropped 60%". If numbers appear, they're the viewer's own, framed as their experiment.
- No "cures", "fixes ADHD" or "rewires your brain". Use "many people find" or "try this".
- Audio: quiet lo-fi or a trending low-key sound. Leave the volume low so the video feels calm, matching the product.
- Screen-record with **Do Not Disturb on** and personal notifications hidden. Use a demo phone layout with no real names or messages.
- CTA: "Kit's in my bio" / "link in bio". On Shorts, pin a comment with the link.

**Recording kit:** iPhone screen recording (Control Centre) plus a second phone filming the hand for the "in-hand" shots. Edit in CapCut. Export 1080×1920.

### 1. Before / after swipe (12 s)
**Hook (0 s):** `my phone before vs after I made it boring`
- 0–2 s: Bright, cluttered home screen with red badges. Thumb hovers.
- 2–3 s: Hard cut on a swipe or snap transition.
- 3–9 s: Dark-grey home screen with 8 graphite icons and one clock widget. Slow pan.
- 9–12 s: Triple-click, and the screen goes greyscale. Text: `same phone. less noise.`
**Caption:** I made my phone boring on purpose. Same apps, way less pull. #minimalistphone #dopaminedetox #homescreen
**CTA (end card):** `wallpapers + icons + setup guide → link in bio`

### 2. "My phone is boring on purpose" (9 s)
**Hook (0 s):** `my phone is boring on purpose`
- 0–3 s: Phone in hand, unlocks to a dark-grey screen. Nothing flashes.
- 3–6 s: Text pops one line at a time: `no colour` / `no badges` / `one page`
- 6–9 s: Phone locks and goes face down on a desk.
**Caption:** If unlocking your phone feels like a slot machine, try making it boring. #lowstim #digitalminimalism
**CTA:** `kit in bio`

### 3. 3 settings that made my phone boring (18 s)
**Hook (0 s):** `3 settings that made my phone boring`
- 0–2 s: Title over the colourful home screen.
- 2–7 s: **1** `Dark icons`: long-press › Edit › Customise › Dark. Icons go dark.
- 7–12 s: **2** `Greyscale on triple-click`: Accessibility › Accessibility Shortcut › Colour Filters, then triple-click.
- 12–16 s: **3** `One Focus, one page`: Focus › Home Screen › select one page. The other pages vanish.
- 16–18 s: Final calm screen. Text: `save this for tonight`
**Caption:** All free and built in. Save it and do it tonight. Full guide + matching wallpapers in bio. #iphonetips #ios26 #minimalist
**CTA:** `full guide → link in bio`

### 4. Satisfying setup timelapse (15 s)
**Hook (0 s):** `turning my phone into a calm phone (timelapse)`
- 0–12 s: 4× speed screen recording. Set wallpaper, switch icons to dark, place custom graphite icons one by one, delete clutter, add one widget.
- 12–15 s: Real-time hold on the finished screen. Slow scroll, nothing else.
**Caption:** 10 minutes, zero colour. #homescreensetup #aesthetic #darkaesthetic
**CTA:** `this exact setup → bio`

### 5. Your screen time experiment (14 s)
**Hook (0 s):** `try this for 7 days and check your own screen time`
- 0–3 s: Screen Time page with the numbers **blurred**. Text: `write down this number`
- 3–9 s: Quick montage of the calm setup going on (dark icons, greyscale, one page).
- 9–12 s: Text: `same phone. 7 days. check again.`
- 12–14 s: Text: `tell me what changed 👇`
**Caption:** Not promising anything, it's your experiment. Screenshot your screen time today, make your phone boring, check in a week. Comment your result. #screentime #dopaminedetox
**CTA:** `setup kit in bio`
*Note: never show real before/after numbers. The viewer supplies their own.*

### 10. "Things I removed from my home screen" (20 s)
**Hook (0 s):** `things I removed from my home screen`
- 0–15 s: Rapid list, each item about 2 s with a matching screen action:

- 15–20 s: Final screen. Text: `what's left is what I actually need`
**Caption:** Subtraction, not willpower. #digitalminimalism #lowstim #phonereset
**CTA:** `the setup kit is in my bio`
