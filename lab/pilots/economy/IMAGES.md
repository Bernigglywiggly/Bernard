# Image prompts: pilot 01, "Why $87,000 Feels Like Less"

33 images, e01 to e33. Common style, append to every prompt:
"pure black background, high-contrast monochrome white-on-black illustration, single subject filling about 70% of the frame, fine engraved linework, no text, no numbers, no logos, no real people, 16:9".

Recurring object: a paper ledger page and a till receipt. People appear only as faceless silhouettes or not at all.
