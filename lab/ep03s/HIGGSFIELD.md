# EP03 cold open · the Higgsfield version (style E)

The same seven beats as the other styles, as photoreal AI video. Higgsfield is blocked from the cloud session, so
run these on the Mac (or the web app), download the MP4s, and put them in `lab/ep03s/plates/` named `E1.mp4`…`E7.mp4`.
I cut them to the audio and add captions and the sound.

Settings for all: 16:9, 1080p, 5 s (E3 and E5 at 8 s), no people, no text in the image, no logos.
Look: graphite and near-black, one turquoise rim light (#12B8AC), chrome and warm gold, shallow depth of field.

|---|---|---|---|---|
