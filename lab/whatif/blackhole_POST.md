# What if Earth fell into a black hole? (What if POV, film 01)

Vertical 1080x1920, 70 s (over a minute, so it counts for TikTok's Creator Rewards). Files (lab/motion/out/):
`whatif_blackhole.mp4` (with our sound design), `whatif_blackhole_silent.mp4` (no audio: add your own TikTok sound),
`whatif_blackhole_sound.mp3` (the sound on its own). Rebuild: `cd lab/motion && npx remotion render WhatIf-BlackHole
out/whatif_blackhole.mp4` (add `--muted` for the silent one); the sound: `python3 lab/whatif/sound_blackhole.py`.

Visuals: 8 GPT Image keyframes from one first-person view, joined by 7 Kling 3.0 clips that start and end on them, so
the whole film reads as one shot. Numbers on screen are real physics (`lab/motion/public/whatif/blackhole/SCIENCE.md`).
